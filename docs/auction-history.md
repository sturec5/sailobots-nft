## Context

Across these lot pages, Sotheby's repeatedly describes Sail-o-Bots as a 750-piece long-form generative art series executed in 2021. Several of the listings are grouped under the recurring "Property from an Iconic Digital Art Collection" and Grails sale framing.

For broader context on the recurring Grails sales, see the official series page:
